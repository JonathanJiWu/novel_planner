    About the method and its sources

    Novel Architect organizes planning with The Unified Novel-Writing Cookbook. Methods credited to Lisa Cron (Story Genius), Jessica Brody (Save the Cat! Writes a Novel), and K.M. Weiland (Structuring Your Novel and the Structuring Your Novel Workbook) are paraphrased summaries of their widely known principles, not quotations. Each stage names the sources it draws on and where the authors differ.

    Questions, exercises, examples, and worksheets are original to this planner; notes marked “Planner synthesis” are its own combination of those ideas. The planner never writes answers for you: every decision, status, and assessment here is yours.
